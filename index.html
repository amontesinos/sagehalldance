New to Country Swing?

First-Timer FAQ

Everything you need to know before stepping out on the dance floor.

🤠 Do I need to bring a partner?

No! In country swing, everyone rotates partners during lessons and social dances. Just show up and you'll find plenty of friendly people to dance with.

👢 What should I wear?

Casual country or athletic wear is great (jeans, t-shirts, flannels). For footwear, leather-soled boots or smooth sneakers make spinning and sliding effortless. Avoid grippy running shoes if possible!

🎓 What if I've never danced before?

You're in the right place! We start every dance night with an introductory lesson covering the basic step, inside turn, and lead/follow connection so you feel confident right away.

💵 How much is entry?

Entry is typically $8 at the door (cash, card, or Venmo accepted). Lessons are included with your general admission!

📍 Why do the locations change?

We partner with premier venues across Logan (Cache County Fairgrounds, Mountain Valley Athletics, and Cache Bar) to bring you massive hardwood dance floors and varied atmospheres throughout the month.

🍺 Are all events 21+?

Most of our dances at Fairgrounds and Mountain Valley Athletics are All Ages (popular with USU students!). Events specifically marked at Cache Bar are 21+ only.
